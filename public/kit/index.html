PROMPT 1 · STRUCTUREIn ChatGPT

Turn your idea into a Build Brief

Answer its questions with real details. You'll get a brief with 8 headings.

I want to build: [your one sentence].
It's for: [who will use it].

Ask me up to 8 short questions, one at a time, to understand what it must do. Then write a Build Brief with these headings:

1. What it is (one sentence)
2. Who it's for
3. The one job it does
4. Must have (no more than 5 things)
5. Not now (what to leave out)
6. Content (the real words, names, prices and links I gave you)
7. Look and feel (3 words and 2 colours)
8. Done when (how I'll know it works)

Keep the brief under 250 words. Don't write any code.

DIRECTINGWith Prompt 3

Talk like an art director

Vague feedback gets random changes. Specific feedback gets exactly what you asked for.

  • Not "make it look better""Make the headline twice the size and the background off-white."
  • Not "make it pop""Make the Order button lime, full width, and fixed to the bottom."
  • Not "make it more modern""Use one font, more space between sections, and rounded corners on the cards."
  • Not "it's not working"Use Prompt 4, with a screenshot.
PROMPT 5 · HAND-OFFIn Claude

Before you close, or when a chat gets long

Write a hand-off note so I can continue this build in a new chat:
the brief, what's done, what's left, and any decisions we made.
Keep it under 200 words.

Fallback briefs

Ready to paste into Claude with Prompt 2. Use one if your own idea isn't working by 3:55. Your idea moves to the 21 days.

FALLBACK A

Portfolio page

Show 3 pieces of your work to a possible client.

1. What it is: A one-page portfolio that shows my best work.
2. Who it's for: Possible clients deciding whether to hire me.
3. The one job it does: Shows 3 pieces of my work and makes it easy to contact me.
4. Must have: My name and a one-line intro. 3 work cards, each with an image, a title, one line about it and a link. A contact button that opens an email or WhatsApp to me.
5. Not now: Blog, prices, testimonials, a contact form, more than 3 projects.
6. Content: Name: [your name]. Intro: [one line about what you do]. Work 1: [title, one line, image link, project link]. Work 2: [title, one line, image link, project link]. Work 3: [title, one line, image link, project link]. Contact: [your email or WhatsApp number].
7. Look and feel: Clean, confident, simple. Colours: [colour 1] and [colour 2].
8. Done when: Someone on a phone can see all 3 pieces of work, open each link, and tap the contact button to reach me.
FALLBACK B

WhatsApp order page

Take food orders for a small business.

1. What it is: A one-page menu where customers pick items and send the order on WhatsApp.
2. Who it's for: Customers of [your business name].
3. The one job it does: Turns a customer's choices into a ready-to-send WhatsApp order.
4. Must have: Menu with prices in naira. Plus and minus buttons for each item. A running total. A name field. An Order on WhatsApp button that opens WhatsApp with the order typed out.
5. Not now: Online payment, accounts, delivery tracking, photos of every item.
6. Content: Business name: [your business name]. WhatsApp number: [international format, no + or spaces, like 2348012345678]. Menu: [item and price, one per line]. Delivery: [areas and fee].
7. Look and feel: Warm, clear, appetising. Colours: [colour 1] and [colour 2].
8. Done when: I can pick 3 items, see the right total, tap the button, and WhatsApp opens with my order typed out.
FALLBACK C

Weekly content planner

Plan a week of social posts.

1. What it is: A one-page planner for one week of social media posts.
2. Who it's for: Me, planning posts for [your brand or page].
3. The one job it does: Shows the week's posts at a glance and what stage each one is at.
4. Must have: 7 day cards, Monday to Sunday. A post idea and a platform for each day. A status for each post: idea, draft or posted. A button to copy the whole week as text.
5. Not now: Logins, scheduling or posting to the platforms, analytics, images.
6. Content: Brand: [your brand or page]. Platforms: [for example Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn]. Post ideas: [one per day, or ask me for ideas].
7. Look and feel: Calm, organised, simple. Colours: [colour 1] and [colour 2].
8. Done when: I can fill in all 7 days, change a status, and copy the week into a message.

Save your build

Do this before you close the tab.

AShare link

If Claude shows a Share or Publish option on your page:

  1. Use it and pick Anyone with the link.
  2. Open the link in a private window to check it works.

BSave the file

Works on any plan.

  1. Ask Claude: "Give me the complete code for this page as one HTML file."
  2. Copy it and paste it into a plain text file.
  3. Save it as my-build.html.
  4. Double-click it to open in Chrome.

Check: close the tab and open your saved copy. If it opens, you're done.

Save your hand-off note (Prompt 5) somewhere you'll find it, so you can continue in a new chat.

Stuck?

Stuck for 3 minutes? Type STUCK in the Zoom chat with a screenshot. You go to the front of the queue.

  • Claude shows code but no pageCheck that code execution and file creation is on in Claude's settings. Then ask: "Show this as a page I can preview."
  • Claude changed things you didn't ask forSay: "Undo that. Go back to the previous version and change only [the one thing]."
  • Made-up text or pricesSay: "Replace all placeholder text with my real content: [paste it]"
  • You hit the free plan limitRun Prompt 5 to get a hand-off note. When the limit resets, start a new chat, paste the note and carry on.
  • The WhatsApp button doesn't openThe number must be in international format with no + or spaces, like 2348012345678.
  • You can't share your screen on a MacOpen System Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Screen and System Audio Recording. Turn on Zoom, then restart Zoom.
